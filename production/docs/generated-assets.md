# Generated asset provenance

Three standalone assets were generated for this production: the single flower, side-profile gazelle, and engraved brass fountain-pen nib. All were produced on uniform white backgrounds, cut out, optimized into WebP, and used as independent material layers in code.

- **Flower:** five-petal terracotta/ochre cut-paper poppy, teal stamens, engraved veins. Divided into five radial masks and a center for analytic bloom motion.
- **Gazelle:** a proportionally correct side-profile sandstone-paper relief. Body and leg regions are animated with conservative joint rotations.
- **Pen nib:** isolated antique brass nib with teal engraving; it introduces the light-thread motif.

No generated image contains required text, poet portraits, or studio logos. Arabic text is native Canvas typography. The studio mark and wordmark are cropped directly from the user-supplied original artwork and segmented into the three original colored lobes. Reference videos are not embedded or reproduced.

Generated full-resolution intermediary PNGs were removed after cutout optimization. The final WebP assets are bundled here so reproduction does not need access to a generation API.
